# OMS — Business Logic Specification

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11.04.2026 | **Status:** Issued for Review

## 1. Partner Rules

- Only partners with compliance status `APPROVED` and a valid agreement may submit intake records (BR-002).
- A partner whose agreement expires is moved to `SUSPENDED`; new intakes are blocked, existing records are unaffected.
- Rejected compliance submissions must include comments.
- Compliance history is retained permanently.

```text
SUBMITTED --> UNDER_REVIEW --> APPROVED --> ACTIVE
                    |                          |
                    +--> REJECTED              +--> SUSPENDED (agreement expired)
                    |                                    |
                    +--> RETURNED --> SUBMITTED          +--> ACTIVE (renewed agreement)
```

## 2. Orphan Intake & Verification

```text
DRAFT --> SUBMITTED --> UNDER_REVIEW --> VERIFIED --> PENDING_APPROVAL --> APPROVED --> REGISTERED
                            |   ^            |                 |
                            |   |            +--(direct)-------+--> APPROVED
                            v   |                              |
                  PENDING_DOCUMENTS /                          +--> RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION
                  RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION --> RESUBMITTED      |
                            |                                  +--> REJECTED
                            +--> REJECTED
```

- Every record must be verified before approval (BR-003).
- After `VERIFIED`, the system checks the reviewer's permission:
  - Has `orphan.approve` → reviewer may approve directly (Scenario A).
  - Otherwise → status `PENDING_APPROVAL`, routed to the Program Manager (Scenario B).
- A partner or submitter can never approve its own record.
- Returned records keep every previous version and comment.
- On `APPROVED`, the system generates the Orphan ID and profile (`REGISTERED`).
- Bulk-imported rows follow the same states; only valid, confirmed rows become `SUBMITTED`.

## 3. Orphan Eligibility

```text
PENDING_ELIGIBILITY_REVIEW --> ELIGIBLE <--> SUSPENDED
            |                     |
            +--> NOT_ELIGIBLE     +--> CLOSED (e.g. age transition, case closure)
```

- Only `REGISTERED` and `ELIGIBLE` orphans enter the matching pool.
- Age transition rules at 18 are pending (P-14); the rule must be configurable.

## 4. Orphan ID

- Generated by the server on approval; never chosen by the user.
- Globally unique, immutable, and never reused.
- Proposed format: `ORP-{COUNTRY}-{YEAR}-{SEQUENCE}` (e.g. `ORP-EG-2026-000142`).
- Contains no personal data.

## 5. Sponsorship Matching

Preconditions (BR-008):

1. At least one sponsor with available sponsorship capacity exists.
2. The orphan is `ELIGIBLE` and has no active sponsorship.

Priority (initial, configurable — P-01):

1. Orphans whose previous sponsorship ended (re-matching priority).
2. Younger orphans.
3. Further parameters (country, waiting time, vulnerability score) as approved.

```text
AVAILABLE_FOR_MATCHING --> MATCHING_PENDING --> MATCHED --> ACTIVE --> EXPIRING_SOON --> RENEWAL_PENDING
                                                              ^                              |
                                                              |                    +---------+---------+
                                                              |                    v                   v
                                                              +-------------- RENEWED        CANCELLATION_REQUESTED
                                                                                                       |
                                                    ON_HOLD <--> ACTIVE                                v
                                                                                                  TERMINATED
                                                                                                       |
                                                                       orphan --> AVAILABLE_FOR_MATCHING (if eligible)
```

- Matching trigger (event-triggered on "Sponsor Available" vs. scheduled) is pending (P-02 / W-11); the engine must support both.
- An orphan cannot have two conflicting active sponsorships.
- Manual override requires the `matching.override` permission and a mandatory reason; it is audited (BR-010, BR-011).

## 6. Renewal

- At the configured threshold (P-03) before `endDate`, status becomes `EXPIRING_SOON` and alerts are sent to the Sponsorship Coordinator and Donor Care.
- Donor Care contacts the sponsor; OMS does not contact donors about renewal (BR-013).
- If Donor Care records no cancellation by the end date, the sponsorship renews automatically (BR-012) and new dates are recorded.
- If cancellation is recorded, the sponsorship is not renewed and moves to termination.

## 7. Termination

- Only a confirmed Donor Care decision can start termination (segregation of duties).
- Termination never deletes sponsorship or financial records (BR-017).
- After termination, the orphan goes to eligibility review; eligible orphans return to the pool with re-matching priority (BR-016).

## 8. General Support Activities

```text
DRAFT --> PENDING_APPROVAL --> APPROVED --> BENEFICIARY_SELECTION --> IN_PROGRESS --> COMPLETED
                                                                                         |
                               CLOSED <-- UNDER_REVIEW <-- REPORT_SUBMITTED <-----------+
                                               |
                                               +--> RETURNED --> REPORT_SUBMITTED
```

- Every activity has a target beneficiary count (BR-005).
- Beneficiaries are selected only from registered orphans.
- Country-specific participation and frequency rules are enforced at selection (BR-006, BR-007).
- Planned vs. actual beneficiaries are tracked; expenditure links to the quarterly report.

## 9. Funds & Disbursement

- Every allocation references a funding source and a purpose (sponsorship, general support, activity, office) (BR-030).
- Amount states per orphan and period: `ALLOCATED → TRANSFERRED → DELIVERED → RECONCILED`; `PENDING = ALLOCATED − DELIVERED`.
- Transferred is never treated as delivered; delivery requires uploaded proof (BR-022).
- Approved disbursements are written to the Orphan Profile (BR-023).

## 10. Quarterly Financial Report

```text
DRAFT --> SUBMITTED --> UNDER_PROGRAM_REVIEW --> PROGRAM_APPROVED --> UNDER_FINANCE_REVIEW --> FINANCE_APPROVED --> FINALIZED
                                |                                              |
                                +--> RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION --> SUBMITTED     +--> REJECTED --> DRAFT
```

- Program Manager approval is first; Central Finance approval is second; both are mandatory (BR-019, BR-020).
- Finance cannot act before program approval.
- A submitter cannot approve their own report.
- Rejection and return require comments; history is retained.

## 11. Annual Progress Report

```text
NOT_STARTED --> PENDING_ASSIGNMENT --> ASSIGNED --> IN_PROGRESS --> SUBMITTED --> UNDER_REVIEW --> APPROVED
                                                                                        |             |
                                                    RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION <-----------+             v
                                                                                               PRIVACY_REVIEW
                                                                                                      |
                                                                    DELIVERY_FAILED <-- SENT <-- READY_FOR_SENDING
```

- On July 1 the system generates a report request for every sponsored orphan (BR-024, BR-025).
- Approval by the Program Manager; privacy review before sending (BR-028).
- Email delivery status is recorded; failures can be retried.
- Indicators are stored per year for historical comparison (BR-026).

## 12. Privacy

The donor version of any report must exclude: national identification numbers, detailed addresses, personal contact information, identity documents, detailed medical records, and other sensitive data unless an approved policy allows it.

## 13. Concurrency and Idempotency

- Matching must lock the orphan and sponsor capacity to prevent double assignment.
- Approval actions must check the current status (optimistic locking) to prevent double approval.
- Email sending and bulk-import confirmation must be idempotent.

## 14. Deletion

Orphan, sponsorship, financial, report, and audit records are never hard-deleted. Records are closed, archived, or marked inactive. Draft intakes may be discarded by their creator before submission.
