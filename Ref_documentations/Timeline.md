# OMS — Project Timeline

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11.04.2026 | **Status:** Indicative

## 1. Planning Baseline

Target initial release: approximately 20 weeks.

| Phase | Duration | Main deliverables |
|---|---:|---|
| Phase 1 — Discovery & Foundation | 2 weeks | Confirm pending decisions, FRS/PRD sign-off, architecture, environments, PMO integration points |
| Phase 2 — Registry & Intake | 4 weeks | Partners & compliance, intake form, bulk import, verification, approval, Orphan ID & profile |
| Phase 3 — Sponsorship | 3 weeks | Sponsors, matching engine, override, renewal, termination, Donor Care exchange |
| Phase 4 — Finance & Field | 4 weeks | Funding, allocation, disbursement & evidence, quarterly reports, general support, field visits |
| Phase 5 — Progress & Reporting | 3 weeks | July cycle, progress reports, privacy review, email delivery, analytics, notifications |
| Phase 6 — QA, UAT & Deployment | 4 weeks | Security, performance, data migration, UAT, training, go-live |

## 2. Milestones

**Week 2:** Requirements baseline and pending decisions approved.

**Week 6:** Intake-to-registration workflow functional.

**Week 9:** Matching, renewal, and termination functional.

**Week 13:** Finance and field workflows functional.

**Week 16:** Progress reporting and donor email functional.

**Week 20:** UAT complete and production release.

## 3. Dependencies

- Confirmation of P-01 – P-17 and W-01 – W-12.
- PMO platform services (SSO, users, documents, notifications).
- Donor Care exchange method.
- Email sender and templates.
- Data migration sources (P-15).
- Representative test data.

## 4. Schedule Control

Any change that materially increases scope is recorded as a change request and evaluated for timeline impact.
