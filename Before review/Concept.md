# OMS — Concept Note

## The idea in simple terms

Instead of tracking orphans in spreadsheets, emails, and paper files, every orphan gets one verified digital profile. Every sponsorship, activity, payment, visit, and progress report is attached to that profile.

Example:

`ORP-EG-2026-000142`

Profile summary:

**REGISTERED — SPONSORED**
Country: Egypt — Field Office: Assiut  
Sponsorship: Annual, active until 06.30.2027  
Delivered this year: 4 of 4 quarterly disbursements (proof attached)  
Annual progress report 2026: Sent to sponsor on 08.21.2026

The system does not decide who deserves support. It applies approved eligibility and matching rules, records every decision, and lets authorized staff override with a recorded reason.
